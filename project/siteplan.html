Site Plan

Our travel agency website will consist of the following pages:

  • Home
  • Destinations
  • Packages
  • Contact Us

Each page will have a consistent design and navigation to ensure a seamless user experience. The Home page will provide an overview of our services, while the Destinations page will showcase popular travel locations. The Packages page will offer various travel packages, and the Contact Us page will allow customers to get in touch with us for inquiries and bookings.
